# Nov 2026 — Leave Approval Revamp

Go-live: 28 Nov 2026
Departments: Sales, Support, Finance, HR
Pass mark: 70

## Stuck approvals move on by themselves

What's new: A request not actioned in 2 working days moves to the approver's manager.
You'll see: An "Escalated to you" tag on the request in the Approvals list.
Instead of: The request sat in the approver's queue until they came back.
Remember: Escalation moves the request to someone else, it never approves it.

## Apply for leave

What's new: Staff see their remaining balance on the form before they send the request.
You'll see: The balance under the date picker, updating as you pick dates.
Steps: Open Leave > pick the dates > check the balance shown > Send > manager gets it in Approvals
Instead of: The balance was only on a separate Leave Balance page.
Remember: The balance already subtracts requests that are still waiting.

## Approve many requests at once

What's new: Managers can approve or reject up to 50 requests in one action.
You'll see: A tick box next to each request, and an Approve selected button.
Steps: Open Approvals > tick the requests > Approve selected > staff get one email each
Instead of: Every request had to be opened and approved one by one.
Remember: One rejection reason applies to the whole batch.
