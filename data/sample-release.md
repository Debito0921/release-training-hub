# Nov 2026 — Leave Approval Revamp

Go-live: 28 Nov 2026
Departments: Sales, Support, Finance, HR
Pass mark: 70

## Feature: Auto-escalation of stuck approvals

What changed: Leave requests not actioned within 2 working days move automatically to the approver's manager.
Why: Staff were waiting weeks when an approver was on leave. This was the top complaint in the last support survey.
Before: A request sat in the approver's queue forever until they came back.
After: After 2 working days the request appears in the next manager's queue, and both people get an email.
Steps:
- No action is needed, the escalation is automatic.
- Approvers see an "Escalated to you" tag on the request.
- The original approver stays visible in the request history.
Misconception: The request is auto-approved after 2 days. || The request is reassigned to another approver, it is never auto-approved.
Misconception: The 2 days are calendar days. || The 2 days are working days, so weekends and public holidays do not count.
Misconception: Support should tell the employee to resubmit a stuck request. || Resubmitting is no longer needed, the request escalates by itself.
Limit: A request does not escalate if the approver has no manager set in the system. HR gets a weekly report of these instead.
Must-know: Escalation reassigns the request, it never approves it.

## Feature: Leave balance shown at submission

What changed: Employees now see their remaining leave balance on the submission form before they send the request.
Why: Around a third of rejected requests were people asking for leave they did not have.
Before: The balance was only visible on a separate Leave Balance page.
After: The balance updates live on the form as the employee picks dates, and blocks submission if the balance is not enough.
Steps:
- Open the leave request form.
- Pick the start and end dates.
- The remaining balance appears under the date picker.
Misconception: The form blocks all requests over the balance. || Unpaid leave and medical leave can still be submitted over the balance, only annual leave is blocked.
Misconception: The balance includes leave that is still pending approval. || The balance already subtracts pending requests, so the number shown is what is really left.
Limit: The live balance does not cover staff on probation, because their entitlement is calculated monthly.
Must-know: The balance shown already subtracts pending requests.

## Feature: Bulk approval for managers

What changed: Managers can select several leave requests and approve or reject them in one action.
Why: Managers with large teams were clicking through requests one by one every Monday.
Before: Each request had to be opened and approved individually.
After: Managers tick the requests in the list and click Approve selected, up to 50 at a time.
Steps:
- Go to the Approvals list.
- Tick the checkbox next to each request.
- Click Approve selected or Reject selected.
Misconception: A rejection reason is optional in bulk mode. || A rejection reason is still required, and it applies to every request in that batch.
Misconception: Bulk approval skips the usual approval rules. || All the normal rules still run, and any request that fails a rule is left untouched and listed as skipped.
Limit: Bulk actions are capped at 50 requests at a time, and are not available on the phone layout.
Must-know: One rejection reason applies to the whole batch, so do not mix unrelated rejections.
